# Changelog

All notable changes to FloatPin are listed here.

## [1.0] - 2026-10-21

The first public release.

- Shake any window to pin it on top, right where you drop it, at the same size.
- A pinned window and the real one stay lined up as one: you see the real window while you're in that app, and the pin floats above everything else the rest of the time.
- Drag or resize a pin and the real window follows underneath.
- Click a pin to jump into the real window, with your click passed through.
- Shake again, click the pin badge, or use the menu bar to unpin.
- ⌃⌥P to pin or unpin the front window.
- Guided setup for Screen Recording and Accessibility.
- Settings for shake sensitivity, click-through, border, frame rate, sounds, and launch at login.
- A pin keeps floating over other windows of the same app too (like a second Chrome window).
